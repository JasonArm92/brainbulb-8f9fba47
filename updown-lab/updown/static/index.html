What's real here

Real

Live BTC/USD prices: the median of Coinbase, Kraken and Bitstamp, updated every second. It stands in for the Chainlink feed Polymarket settles on, which isn't public. Window outcomes, the model, its backtest and the GBP rate are real too.

Simulated

The market: the crowd's prices and the traders who sell to us. Profit here depends on those assumptions. It shows how well the hedging manages risk, not what a real market would pay.

Not possible from the UK

Real trading. Polymarket and Kalshi block UK residents, and the FCA bans binary options for retail. There is no live-trading code in this project.
