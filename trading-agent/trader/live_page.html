Safety limits

Hard rules the bot cannot break. If the account falls 15% from its best, or 3% in one day, it stops trading by itself. It can hold at most 2 coins, never bets more than 30% on one, never borrows and never bets on prices falling (not allowed for UK retail).

UK tax record

Selling crypto is a "disposal" for Capital Gains Tax. The bot keeps an HMRC-style record using the same-day, 30-day and pooled-cost rules, with fees counted. The first £3,000 of net gains each tax year (6 April to 5 April) is tax-free. Practice trades aren't taxable; this is so the real account is ready. Not tax advice.
